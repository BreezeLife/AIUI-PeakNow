# Studio and device acceptance

Record the actual Rokid model, phone model, companion-host build, runtime version,
permission state and sensor origin before assigning a physical result.

1. Import `/` from the stated GitHub ref in AIUI Studio on AIUI 0.17.0. Open the Page
   full-screen and embedded. Confirm cover, five active seconds, tap start, and
   language selection before start.
2. Check English, Japanese, Korean and Chinese text on both surfaces. Test long
   local summit names, absent translation and unknown elevation. Confirm Prev/Next,
   Refresh, settings and Back remain visible.
3. Permit and deny location, then revoke it later. Verify explicit state and retry.
   Confirm no demo is substituted. Test manual coordinates and agent launch data.
4. Verify the host's Accelerometer and Magnetometer originate from the intended
   phone/companion device. Turn the phone, hold it steadily in the intended direction,
   and compare several headings with a known reference. Check magnetic interference
   and unsupported/error delivery. Treat unverified origin or mounting as blocked.
5. Walk outdoors: verify GPS course appears only with adequate speed and accuracy.
   Stop or lose delivery: verify it expires. Set manual direction, turn your body,
   and verify the label continues to say Manual until you change it.
6. Download a real mountain area. Switch views and languages offline; refresh during
   failure; confirm cached rows remain visible with the error. Move outside full
   cache coverage, change radius/filter, and retry after cooldown.
7. Hide, blur, show and reopen repeatedly. Verify sensors, location watches and
   network requests stop, late callbacks are ignored, and resumed sessions are unique.
8. Check real temple tap/Enter, direction controls and host Back at the root. Confirm
   visible focus and unfocused input handling. Measure cold start and continuous use.
9. Inspect bright, dark and cluttered backgrounds through the glasses. Check readable
   text, optical comfort, safe insets and line weight. Radar is a diagram; validate
   that users understand it does not track gaze or prove mountain visibility.

Capture current-source Studio/device evidence using the skill's separate authority
roles. The local browser and deterministic tests do not establish these results.
